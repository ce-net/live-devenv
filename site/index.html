A workspace made of cells.

One workspace shared by people and agents on three nodes. Files, builds, tests, tools, reviews and the record of who did what are cells with typed contracts. No op takes a command line: every edit and run is checked against the caller's capability chain, runs in a box, and leaves a signed, hash-linked receipt.
